# Input Assist

Shifts and buffers your clicks by whole physics ticks (1 tick = 1/240 s).

- **Click Delay**: every click and release lands N ticks later. Useful if you consistently click a little early.
- **Buffer Ticks**: every press is held for at least N ticks, so a tap that ends just before you land or reach an orb still counts.

Everything is adjustable live from the mod settings, including from the pause menu. Inputs only ever move later and onto tick boundaries, so physics stays deterministic in normal and practice mode.

**Note:** this changes gameplay timing. Completions made with it enabled count as hacked on community lists.
